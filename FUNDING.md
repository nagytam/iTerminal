# Support iTerminal

iTerminal is free and MIT licensed, and it will stay that way. It is built mostly in spare time by
a single maintainer, with AI assistance for design, implementation, test writing and review. That
assistance has a real monthly cost. If iTerminal saves you or your team time, please consider
helping to pay for it.

## How to sponsor

- **GitHub Sponsors:** `<SPONSOR URL>`
- One-off or monthly, every amount helps. Companies can sponsor on behalf of a team.
- **Invoices and in-kind help** (API credits, CI minutes, a code-signing certificate, package
  hosting) are welcome too. Write to [mail@tamas-nagy.net](mailto:mail@tamas-nagy.net).

## What the money pays for

1. **AI budget:** model usage and tooling used to implement features, write tests, review code and keep the docs current.
2. **Maintainer time:** faster pull-request review, issue triage and releases.
3. **Infrastructure:** CI, release builds, code signing and package distribution (MSI, deb, rpm, and package-manager listings).
4. **Contributor bounties** (later, once funds allow): payment for issues tagged for it.

## Tiers

Amounts and perks below are a starting point. See the sponsor page for the current list.

| Tier | For | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supporter | Individuals | Thanks, your name in [SPONSORS.md](SPONSORS.md) if you wish, a sponsor badge on your GitHub profile |
| Backer | Daily users | Everything above, plus a vote in roadmap polls |
| Team | Small companies | Your logo in the README, a mention in release notes, priority triage of your issues |
| Partner | Companies and vendors | A larger logo, a short case study or blog post, a quarterly call with the maintainer |
| In-kind | AI, CI and signing providers | Credit and a mention for your credits or services, treated like money in reports |

Sponsorship does not buy control of the project. The licence stays MIT, and priority triage does
not mean a guaranteed fix or delivery date.

## Transparency

A short note is published each month with what was received, how it was spent (AI budget,
infrastructure, maintainer time) and what shipped, posted in the repository's Discussions or
release notes.

## Other ways to help

Money is not the only way. You can:

- Star the repository and tell a colleague.
- [Report a bug or send a pull request](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Translate the interface into your language.
- Write about iTerminal, or give a talk about it.
